01  /  About

Two skillsets, one mindset.

Clinical judgment backed by systems logic. I treat repetitive work as a bug and write the fix.

I didn’t start in pharmacy. I started by breaking things — reverse-engineering spreadsheets, building trading bots, and figuring out why automated systems fail.

That instinct followed me through pharmacy school and into the clinic. Today I run the operations layer of a US telehealth clinic, and I build the tooling that keeps it moving: a 45+ script suite of Tampermonkey userscripts and Manifest V3 Chrome extensions covering intake, order queues, tracking, and address validation across a CRM and seven pharmacy portals.

I’m not naturally a detail-oriented person — my own assessments say so. Automation and checklists are how I compensate, and it’s exactly why I notice when a workflow is only holding together because someone remembers to do something.
